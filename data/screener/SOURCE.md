# Screener.in sector exports (single source of truth for the universe)

Downloaded 26-Sep-2026 from screener.in sector pages, all rows, one file per sector:

- `capital_goods.csv`: Capital Goods (887 companies, all five industry groups)
- `power.csv`: Power (49 companies)
- `cement.csv`: Cement & Cement Products (42 companies)

Universe rule agreed with the group: NSE-listed, market cap >= Rs 5,000 cr, at least one year of
trading history; Capital Goods without Aerospace & Defense, Packaging, Rubber, Glass, Aluminium/Copper/
Zinc products, commercial vehicles, tractors and vehicle dealers. Not yet wired into the pipeline:
the portfolio is re-selected from this universe once the group agrees the list.
